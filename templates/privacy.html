Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run the service: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to produce summaries such as category breakdowns and date-filtered totals. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database associated with the Spendly application. Passwords are never stored in plain text. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may send standard request information, such as your IP address, to Google when a page is displayed.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team. Contact details will be published here once available.
